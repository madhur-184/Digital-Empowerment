## Problem Statement
Many people in rural areas use smartphones but are unaware of:
- Digital Literacy
- Cyber Security
- Artificial Intelligence
- Fake News
- Financial Literacy
Due to this lack of awareness, villagers are more vulnerable to cyber fraud, misinformation, and unsafe digital practices.

## Objectives
- Create awareness about digital technologies.
- Educate villagers on cyber safety.
- Explain Artificial Intelligence using simple examples.
- Help people identify fake news.
- Promote safe digital payment practices.
- Encourage responsible use of technology.

## Why is this Project Needed ?
- Increase digital awareness in villages.
- Reduce cybercrime and online fraud.
- Prevent the spread of fake news.
- Improve financial literacy.
- Help villagers use government digital services safely.
- Build confidence in using digital technology.

## Expected Outcomes
- Improved digital awareness among villagers.
- Better understanding of cyber security.
- Awareness about Al applications.
- Ability to identify fake news.
- Safer use of digital payment methods.
- Increased confidence in using smartphones for useful services.
